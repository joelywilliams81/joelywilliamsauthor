The Poetry Letter Club

Handwritten poems by mail. Tell me what you want to see, and I write toward it. You can write back any way you like, or not at all.

What arrives

Handmade from old book pages.

  • A handwritten letter, written to what you asked for
  • A handmade zine of fresh poems
  • A tea bag
  • A journaling prompt
  • A word card for your next poem
  • Ekphrastic poem inspiration
  • A rough draft image
  • A trinket picked with you in mind
